gameplay
=============================
- The radiation fabricator collects hawking radiation to create new objects
- The engineer with the help of materials you give him is able to improve the design of it
- Radiation Fabricator MKII-whateverthefuck can make better items and with better effiecency
- You need some of these crafting reciepes to fix the engineer's ship (see story)
- The black hole occasionally spits out items to boost the player's progress 
	(some are going to be forced to allow progression without rng)
- All the plants and other living things got sucked out into the vaccum and the black hole during the breach so food is limited as well as oxygen
- The engineer has one hour gameplay of oxygen and food for both of you
- The fabricator mkII or mkIII (figure it out later) can make these
- mkIV or V can make all the repairs to fix the ship

story
=============================
- humanity has been leaching off of a dying black hole for years now
- the black hole has started to run out of mass and is ejecting larger and larger objects because its' gravity can't hold it in
- as a last resort, you the player, has to save humanity at the end of time by sending them back to the time of light
- you use resources to try to create a time machine to go back
- if you fail to make it in time you watch the black hole explode as the last act of the universe and time continues without end
	- alternate ending being that god resets the universe (returning player to menu) with "Let there be Light"
- success is achieved by going back to the age of starlight by creating a time machine that can handle the travel
- trying to leave your current time too early can cause you to fall into an aybss outside of time.
