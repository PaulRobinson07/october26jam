=============================
story
=============================
- humanity has been leaching off of a dying black hole for years now
- the black hole has started to run out of mass and is ejecting larger and larger objects because its' gravity can't hold it in
- as a last resort, you the player, has to save humanity at the end of time by sending them back to the time of light
- you use resources to try to create a time machine to go back
- if you fail to make it in time you watch the black hole explode as the last act of the universe and time continues without end
	- alternate ending being that god resets the universe (returning player to menu) with "Let there be Light"
- success is achieved by going back to the age of starlight by creating a time machine that can handle the travel
- trying to leave your current time too early can cause you to fall into an aybss outside of time. 
	- maybe some weird graphical effects or something
